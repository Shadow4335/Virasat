Our Story

Virasat means legacy.

The Nehru jacket has stood for quiet confidence for generations. We honour that heritage — the mandarin collar, the clean line, the dignity — and rework it for a wardrobe that moves between continents and occasions.

Each piece blends Indian motifs and embroidery with the structure of Western tailoring: zippered fronts, welt pockets, textured weaves. The result is a jacket that feels inherited, yet entirely new.
